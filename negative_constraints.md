# negative_constraints.md
## Global Negative Constraints（全局负面约束，每次生成角色板必须追加在Prompt末尾）
no missing expression sheet, no fewer than four expression panels, no unlabeled expression panels, no changing face, no changing expression identity, no changing hairstyle, no changing hair color, no removing marks/accessories, no changing outfit, no changing accessories, no changing color palette, no changing material, no changing body type, no changing art style, no invented core props, no extra characters, no inconsistent turnaround, no inconsistent face between views, no missing front/side/back views, no unreadable layout, no decorative clutter, no watermark, no logo, no fake UI, no polaroid frame, no photo-of-a-photo effect.

### 中文说明
- 禁止省略表情四格EXPRESSION SHEET区块
- 禁止修改角色五官脸型、发型发色
- 禁止移除角色身上的标记、饰品、服装
- 禁止篡改原图配色、材质、体型和原作画风
- 禁止凭空创造不存在的道具
- 禁止增加额外人物
- 三视图必须保持面部统一，不能崩坏
- 禁止水印、logo、相框、照片效果
